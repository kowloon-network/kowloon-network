## Who to ask

Every Kowloon community runs on its own server, with its own admins. **For anything about your account or your community** -- you can't sign in, you need an invitation, something someone posted, a report you made -- ask your server's admins first. They're the ones who can help.

**For a problem with the app itself** -- it crashes, something doesn't work, or you have an idea -- [open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/kowloon-network/kowloon-mobile/issues), or email **support@kowloon.network**.

## Common questions

**How do I join?** Kowloon communities are invitation-only. Ask someone who's already a member, or the server's admins, for an invite. Each invitation is a link or a QR code. In the app, tap **Scan invite code**, or tap **Register** and paste the code.

**I forgot my password.** On the sign-in screen, tap **Forgot your password?** and enter your Kowloon ID (such as `@you@example.com`) and the email address on your account. Your server will send you a reset link.

**How do I download my data?** Go to **Settings -> Download Your Data**. You'll get a zip file of your posts, files and settings.

**How do I delete my account?** Go to **Settings -> Delete Account** and confirm with your password. Your account and what you've posted are removed from your server straight away.

**How do I report someone or something?** Tap the **...** menu on a post, reply, profile or group and choose **Report**. Your server's moderators review every report.

**How do I block or mute someone?** Open their profile and tap **...**. Blocked people can't see your posts or reply to you; muted people's posts stay out of your feeds without them knowing.

**How do I stop seeing certain kinds of posts?** Go to **Settings -> Preferences -> Content warnings** and choose show, warn or hide for each kind of content.
