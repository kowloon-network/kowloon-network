_Last updated 17 November 2026_

This policy covers the **Kowloon app** for iPhone and Android. It's short because the app does very little with your information on its own.

## The short version

- The app is a window onto the **Kowloon server you sign in to**. Your account, posts, photos and settings live on that server, and its own privacy policy covers them. Every Kowloon server publishes one, linked from its sign-up page.
- The people who make the app don't run an account system, show ads, sell data, or use analytics or tracking. The app doesn't follow you around other apps or websites.
- A few things leave your phone for somewhere other than your server. They're all listed below, with why.

## Where your information goes

**Your Kowloon server.** Everything you do in the app -- posting, reading, replying, changing settings -- is a conversation between the app and the server you signed in to. The server's admins can see what any server's admins can: your account details and what you post.

**Crash reports.** When the app crashes or hits an error, it sends a report to errors.kowloon.network, an error tracker we run ourselves (not a third-party service). A report contains your device model, its operating system and app version, and what the app was doing when it went wrong. Before a report leaves your phone, the app removes the addresses it was visiting and their search terms, invitation and password-reset codes, and anything it printed to its log. Reports don't include your name, email address or posts. We use them only to fix bugs, and delete them after 90 days.

**Notifications.** If you allow notifications, your server sends them through Expo's notification service, which passes them to Apple or Google to reach your phone. Each notification carries the other person's display name and a short description, such as "replied to your post". Your server keeps a device code so it knows where to send them; it's deleted when you sign out or delete your account.

**Location.** The app only uses your location when you tap **Use current location** while adding a place to a post. Your phone's position, or the place name you type into the search box, is sent to OpenStreetMap's place-name service (Nominatim) to find the place. The place you pick is then shown on that post, to whoever the post is shared with. The app never tracks your location in the background.

**Photos, video and audio.** The app only sees the files you choose to post. Your server removes location data that phones hide inside photos and videos. The camera is only used to scan invitation QR codes; nothing is recorded or kept.

**Embedded videos.** When you play a YouTube video in a post, it loads from YouTube's privacy-enhanced service (youtube-nocookie.com), under YouTube's privacy policy.

**On your phone.** The app keeps your sign-in in your phone's secure storage, and stores your reading settings and drafts. All of it is removed when you sign out or delete the app.

## Your choices

- **Download your data:** in the app, go to **Settings -> Download Your Data**.
- **Delete your account:** go to **Settings -> Delete Account**. It removes your account and content from your server. Copies of public posts that other Kowloon servers have already received may stay on those servers, as with an email you've already sent.
- You can turn off notifications, location and camera access at any time in your phone's settings. The app still works without them.

## Children

The app isn't intended for children under 13, and each Kowloon server can set its own minimum age.

## Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date at the top. Questions go to **support@kowloon.network**.
